Protocol

Requested API model: anthropic/claude-haiku-5.5. Reasoning and sampling use provider defaults without overrides; the output limit is 16,384 tokens per response. Four concurrent tasks per harness; Linux/bubblewrap isolation and a host-owned trusted service. DeepAgents forwards reasoning history and enables its native subagents; mini-SWE-agent uses its native DefaultAgent. The base budget is 160 graph supersteps for DeepAgents or 160 model calls for mini-SWE-agent, raised by numeric task-specific floors. These units are different. Ordinary execution has a 3,600-second guard, long-context tasks retain their 7,200-second floor, and selected coupled workflows retain their 900-second limit and 320 service requests. Setup and verification are outside execution limits.

Accounting and limits

Published per-task outcomes and counters come from final retained attempts only. Superseded attempts and separate smoke checks are excluded. Counters are native recorded subtotals, not complete API spend: mini-SWE-agent has steps and tokens for 88/90 tasks; DeepAgents has steps for 88/90 and tokens for 89/90. Missing counters are null in the downloadable data. Request-level completeness has not been independently audited; the leaderboard marks these totals partial. Source fingerprints identify the private final artifacts and protocol without publishing raw prompts, responses, credentials or internal endpoints.

One run per harness; no independent repeats or repeat-variability interval. The observed difference is three tasks, not evidence of causal harness superiority. Historical measurements keep their original scopes and profiles.
